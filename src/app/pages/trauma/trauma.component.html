Colorado's Trusted Trauma & PTSD Treatment Center

Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) is a mental health condition caused by an extremely stressful or terrifying event. Symptoms may include flashbacks, nightmares, severe anxiety, and uncontrollable thoughts about the event. At Circle Psychiatry, one of Colorado's leading trauma and PTSD treatment centers, we provide specialized care that meets you where you are.

If symptoms last for months or years and affect the ability to function daily, professional treatment is available and effective. Trauma and PTSD affect school-aged children, teenagers, and adults of all ages — and our Colorado trauma treatment center is equipped to support all of them with evidence-based, individualized care.

You are not alone, and healing is possible. With specialized treatment and compassionate support, individuals recover from trauma and go on to rebuild their lives.
